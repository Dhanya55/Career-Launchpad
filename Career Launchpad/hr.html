Program Overview

What you'll actually be doing

This internship places you inside an active HR function, working alongside recruiters and people-ops professionals on tasks that matter. You'll learn how companies attract talent, evaluate candidates, and build the systems that keep employees engaged and productive — all while getting direct feedback from mentors who do this for a living.

By the end of the program, you'll have hands-on experience across the full employee lifecycle, a portfolio of real HR deliverables, and a verified certificate to show for it.

Full Employee Lifecycle Real Deliverables Mentor Feedback

Day-to-day, you'll:

Screen live candidate applications against real role requirements.

Sit in on hiring manager syncs and interview debriefs.

Draft onboarding materials new hires actually use.

Get weekly 1:1 feedback from your assigned HR mentor.
