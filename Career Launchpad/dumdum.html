Program Overview

What you'll actually be doing

This internship rotates you through three connected product functions — product management, web & app development, and UX/UI design — working alongside the people who take an idea from a rough sketch to a shipped feature. You'll see how a feature gets scoped, how it gets designed and prototyped, and how it gets built and shipped into a real product.

By the end of the program, you'll have hands-on experience across the full product lifecycle, a portfolio of real deliverables from all three functions, and a verified certificate to show for it.

Full Product Lifecycle Real Deliverables Mentor Feedback

Day-to-day, you'll:

Research and scope real feature requirements with product managers.

Sit in on sprint planning, design reviews, and standups.

Write code, build UI, and ship features into a real codebase.

Get weekly 1:1 feedback from mentors across all three tracks.
