Program Overview

What you'll actually be doing

This internship rotates you through three connected functions — data science, AI & machine learning, and automation — working alongside the people who turn raw data into working products. You'll see how a dataset gets cleaned and explored, how a model gets built and evaluated, and how AI and automation get deployed into real workflows that run without manual work.

By the end of the program, you'll have hands-on experience across the full data-to-deployment pipeline, a portfolio of real deliverables from all three functions, and a verified certificate to show for it.

Data-to-Deployment Pipeline Real Deliverables Mentor Feedback

Day-to-day, you'll:

Clean, explore, and visualize real datasets alongside data scientists.

Sit in on model reviews, standups, and automation demos.

Train models, build AI agents, and automate real workflows.

Get weekly 1:1 feedback from mentors across all three tracks.
