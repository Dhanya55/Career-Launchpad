Program Overview

What you'll actually be doing

This internship rotates you through three connected growth functions — business development, marketing, and sales — working alongside the people who own the whole customer journey, from first outreach to signed deal. You'll see how a lead gets identified, how a campaign builds demand around it, and how a pitch turns interest into revenue.

By the end of the program, you'll have hands-on experience across the full growth funnel, a portfolio of real deliverables from all three functions, and a verified certificate to show for it.

Full Growth Funnel Real Deliverables Mentor Feedback

Day-to-day, you'll:

Research and qualify prospects against real growth criteria.

Sit in on campaign syncs, discovery calls, and pipeline reviews.

Draft outreach, campaign assets, and pitch decks teams actually use.

Get weekly 1:1 feedback from mentors across all three tracks.
